## Suggested Itinerary — 3-Day Trip from Tulsa

**Day 1: South Across the Red River**
- Leave Tulsa in the morning
- Cross into Texas, lunch in the DFW area or Waco
- Arrive in Meadow Springs by late afternoon (or overnight in Austin/Waco to split the drive)
- Dinner downtown — German food, Texas BBQ, or craft beer

**Day 2: Chocolate, Wine, and Hill Country**
- Morning: Wine & Chocolate Tasting at Highland Chocolate Co.
- Midday: Visit 2–3 wineries along the local Wine Road
- Afternoon: Hike the nearby granite dome or explore the shops downtown
- Dinner: Farm-to-table Hill Country dining with sunset views

**Day 3: Last Morning, Then North**
- Brunch in Meadow Springs
- Stock up on chocolate for the drive home
- Head north — or split the return drive with a stop in Dallas or Austin

**Where to Stay**
Meadow Springs has hundreds of guest houses, B&Bs, and vacation rentals, including historic German-style cottages — a charming change from anything you'll find in Oklahoma. Book early for spring weekends.

**Fly-In Option**
Fly into Austin (AUS) or San Antonio (SAT), rent a car, and you're in Meadow Springs in about 90 minutes. Major carriers serve both airports from Tulsa.

## FAQ

**Is it worth the 7.5-hour drive?**
Absolutely — especially if you break it up. Many Tulsa visitors fly into Austin or San Antonio (90 min away) to save time. But the drive through Texas is its own adventure.

**Do I need a reservation for the tasting?**
Yes — tastings are limited to 10 guests. Reserve at least a week ahead, especially during spring and holiday weekends.

**Can I fly instead of drive?**
Yes. Austin (AUS) and San Antonio (SAT) are both about 90 minutes away. Fly in, rent a car, and skip the full drive.

**What else is there to do nearby?**
Dozens of wineries, a well-known granite dome hike, a historic downtown with shopping and dining, a regional history museum, and hundreds of guesthouses. Most visitors wish they'd planned more days.

**Best time of year for this trip?**
Spring (March–May) is wildflower season and the Hill Country is at its most beautiful. Fall has perfect weather. Winter is mild and quiet. Summer is hot, but the tasting room and wineries are cool inside.
